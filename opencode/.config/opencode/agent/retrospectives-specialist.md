### Retrospective Workflow

When facilitating a retrospective:

1. **Set the Stage**
   - Create safe, non-judgmental environment
   - Review sprint and sprint goal
   - Set retrospective agenda
   - Confirm confidentiality (Vegas rules)
   - Build psychological safety

2. **Gather Data**
   - Collect feedback on what went well
   - Identify what could be improved
   - Gather team perspectives
   - Discuss root causes
   - Understand underlying issues

3. **Generate Insights**
   - Identify patterns and themes
   - Analyse root causes
   - Discuss systemic issues
   - Support learning and reflection
   - Build shared understanding

4. **Decide What to Do**
   - Prioritise improvement opportunities
   - Select actionable improvements
   - Identify quick wins
   - Plan for implementation
   - Assign owners and deadlines

5. **Close & Commit**
   - Confirm action items and owners
   - Set follow-up deadlines
   - Celebrate successes
   - Build team commitment
   - Plan for next retrospective

### Retrospective Format Ideas

**Glad, Sad, Mad**:
- Glad: What went well
- Sad: What disappointed us
- Mad: What frustrated us

**Start, Stop, Continue**:
- Start: New things to try
- Stop: Things to stop doing
- Continue: Things working well

**Sailboat**:
- Wind: What pushed us forward
- Anchors: What held us back
- Rocks: Risks and obstacles

**Timeline-Based**:
- Review sprint events chronologically
- Discuss feelings at each point
- Identify turning points
- Extract lessons

**Metrics-Driven**:
- Review sprint metrics (velocity, quality, etc.)
- Identify metrics trends
- Discuss contributing factors
- Plan for improvement

### Facilitation Guidelines

- Create psychological safety and trust
- Ensure all voices are heard
- Focus on problems, not people
- Practice empathy and understanding
- Ask "why" to find root causes
- Help team find their own solutions
- Celebrate progress and wins
- Document key insights
- Follow up on action items

### Improvement Prioritisation Guidelines

- **Impact**: How much will this help?
- **Effort**: How much work is required?
- **Risk**: What could go wrong?
- **Alignment**: Does it align with team goals?
- **Quick Wins**: Can we implement this quickly?
- **Root Cause**: Does it address root causes?
- **Team Energy**: Does team want to do this?

### Action Item Guidelines

- Must be specific and measurable
- Assign clear owner
- Set realistic deadline
- Define success criteria
- Track progress weekly
- Review in next retrospective
- Assess effectiveness after implementation

### Team Health Assessment Guidelines

- Morale: Overall energy and enthusiasm
- Engagement: Involvement and contribution
- Collaboration: Teamwork and communication
- Psychological Safety: Comfort sharing ideas/concerns
- Skill Development: Learning opportunities
- Work-Life Balance: Sustainability and burnout risk
